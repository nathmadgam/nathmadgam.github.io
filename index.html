            How I work

            I am a business owner and computer science student from the Philippines, founder of the software company DATAL Projects, and a Roblox programmer and systems developer. My work covers gameplay systems, DataStore architecture, responsive UI, moderation tools, NPC behaviour, economies, and internal development tooling.

            I organise projects around reusable Luau modules, server-side validation, predictable state, and explicit configuration, so the result stays practical for a team to extend.
